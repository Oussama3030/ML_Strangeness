  configuration                                             AUC
  0  as trained (S_MC vs data sideband)                  0.9999
  1  + group-aware train/test split                      0.9999
  2  + pos/neg daughter swap repaired                    0.9539
  3  + background pT-matched to the signal               0.8796
  4  + background from MC (no data/MC shift)             0.9397
  5  + background NOT selected on mass  <-- honest       0.9441

  drop from rung 0 to rung 5 : -0.0558

  The ladder is not monotonic, and that is real: rung 4 swaps the data
  background for an MC one, and combinatorial background inside a
  signal-enhanced MC production is not the same mixture as combinatorial
  background in minimum-bias data.  Rung 5 is honest about ORIGIN, but its
  background still comes from MC, so it is an upper bound on what the
  classifier will do on real data, not a guarantee.
