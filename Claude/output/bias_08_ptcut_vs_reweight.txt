WHAT THE NUMBERS SAY
==============================================================================
  At a 4 GeV/c cut:
    signal left        : 327 of 1396 candidates (77% thrown away)
    spectra overlap    : 0.538  (1 = identical shapes)
    AUC cut only       : 0.8406
    AUC cut + reweight : 0.7605   <- the cut alone leaves +0.080
    AUC placebo        : 0.6873   <- still no physics in it

  For comparison, no cut at all but reweighted: 0.8675

  A maximum-pT cut fixes the RANGE but not the SHAPE.  Truncating a flat
  spectrum at 4 GeV/c leaves a flat spectrum in [0, 4]; the data background
  is still falling steeply across that same window, so "hard or soft?"
  remains available and the placebo stays well above 0.5.

  It also costs most of the signal, and it costs it exactly where the
  analysis is most interesting: after the cut the model has never seen a
  high-pT L*, so it cannot be applied above the cut at all.

  Reweighting keeps every candidate and matches the shape, so it dominates
  the cut on both axes.  If a cut is wanted anyway, the honest version is a
  LOWER bound as well -- train inside a window where both samples have
  statistics -- or, better, train in pT bins, which the task already
  supports (it plots AUC vs pT).
