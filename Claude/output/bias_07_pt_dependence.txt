==============================================================================
READING
==============================================================================
  fOPAngle is the extreme case: a two-body opening angle goes like ~m/p, so
  it is almost a pure momentum variable, AND it is the variable the mass
  sideband cut was made on (bias_04).  It is doing double duty as a bias.

  fLambdaRadius and fLambdaLifeTime scale with the boost, so a harder signal
  decays further out.  That is real physics, but here it separates the
  classes only because the two samples sit at different momenta.

  fPhotonRadius is set by where the material is, not by the momentum, so it
  stays flat -- which is exactly why it is one of the safer features.
  The Armenteros variables (Qt, Alpha) are bounded by the decay kinematics
  and are likewise close to momentum-blind.
